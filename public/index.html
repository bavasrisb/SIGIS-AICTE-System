Cross-Bureau Overlap & Multi-Channel Duplicate Detection

Whistleblowers and aggrieved students frequently file identical grievances across separate AICTE bureaus (e.g., SGRC, Anti-Ragging, Approval Bureau, Regional Bureau, PGRC). The SIGIS Overlap Engine computes TF-IDF text similarity, category correlation, and AICTE Permanent Institution ID anchoring to uncover coordination-required cases.

--
Potential Pairs Flagged
Human Review Policy: A flagged pair represents “Potential Overlapping Cases – Coordination Required”, not an automatic duplicate. To uphold evidentiary integrity, complaints are never automatically merged, deleted, or rejected without authorized human officer adjudication.
